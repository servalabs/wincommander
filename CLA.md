## Why ServaLabs needs the relicensing right

WinCommander uses a **dual-license model**:

- **Free tier** (`commander-free`, `wincmd-shared`): GNU Affero
  General Public License v3.0 — OSI-approved open source with
  copyleft + network-use clause.
- **Pro / Cleanup tier** (`commander-pro`): governed by the
  [WinCommander EULA](https://servalabs.com/eula) — private repo,
  proprietary, binary-gated
  by a paid licence key.

ServaLabs may change either licence at any time — for example:

- Moving the Free tier to a different permissive or copyleft OSS
  licence (MIT, MPL 2.0, AGPL, etc.).
- Strengthening the Pro tier to a stricter proprietary licence, or
  relaxing it to open source if the business model evolves.
- Adopting a Business Source Licence (BSL) or similar time-delayed
  model for future features.

Without an explicit, irrevocable relicensing right from each
contributor, re-licensing any portion of the codebase requires
getting every contributor's consent — which becomes impractical as
the contributor base grows. The CLA solves this once, upfront.

This is the same model used by HashiCorp, Elastic, MongoDB, and most
mature open-core businesses before and after licence changes.

**The current commitment to open-source for the Free tier remains.**
But we want the legal option to evolve the model without becoming
hostage to it.

## How to sign

**Currently** (no CLA-assistant bot wired up yet): open a pull
request and paste the following into your PR description — we'll
record it manually:

> I, @your-github-username, have read the WinCommander CLA at
> [CLA.md](CLA.md) and agree to its terms for this and any future
> contributions to this repository.

**Once the CLA-assistant bot is wired up:** it will comment on your
pull request with a link to confirm acceptance — you'll click
through, sign in with your GitHub account, and the bot records your
acceptance. After that, all future PRs from your GitHub account are
auto-recognised as covered. This page will be updated once that's
live.

For the corporate CLA (employer-on-behalf), email
legal@servalabs.com.
